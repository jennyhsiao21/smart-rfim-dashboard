The Problem

Most merchants ran customer segmentation in a spreadsheet — or not at all.

Across 15,000 enterprise merchants on the SHOPLINE engagement platform, only the most sophisticated had data teams running RFM analyses. The other 90% had untapped revenue sitting in dormant cohorts and no scalable way to reach them.

⚙

Manual workflows didn't scale

Top-decile merchants segmented customers in Excel. The bottom 90% relied on broad blasts to all customers, watching unsubscribe rates climb and conversion fall.

⏱

Industry RFM ignored social

Classic Recency–Frequency–Monetary models treat every customer who hasn't purchased as silent. But social-commerce customers leave signals: livestream comments, post engagements, DMs. RFM left those unused.

📉

Activation was disconnected from segmentation

Even merchants who built segments couldn't act on them — the marketing tool, the rewards tool, and the export tool were three separate workflows. Segments became reports, not levers.

The 9-Segment Model

RFM is industry standard. RFIM was the SHOPLINE innovation.

We added an Interaction layer — social engagements (livestream comments, post replies, social DMs, in-app browse) — because in social commerce, engagement precedes purchase. Segments are computed per-merchant: a "Top Shopper" for a small fashion brand is scored against that merchant's distribution, not a global one.

Top Shopper

Highest recency × frequency × monetary. The 1% you protect at all costs.

R1 · F1 · M1

Loyal Shopper

Repeat customers who haven't hit Top status yet. The growth pipeline.

R≤2 · F≤2 · M≤2

Active Customer

Currently transacting. Watch frequency to graduate to Loyal.

R≤2 · F≤3

Engaged Customer

Interacting socially even without recent purchase — the social-commerce wedge.

High I, mixed RFM

Ready to Repurchase

Hit usual repurchase cycle. The highest-ROI broadcast cohort.

R=2 · F≤2

New Shopper

First-time buyers. Onboarding is the differentiator between this and Active.

R=1 · F≥3

Potential Shopper

High interaction, no purchase. Social followers waiting for the right offer.

High I · F=4

Sleeping Customer

Was active, has gone quiet. The reactivation wedge — Bonny & Read's $500K week.

R≥3 · F≥3

At-Risk / Lost

Long quiet, low value. Test before investing in reactivation.

R=4 · F=4
How It Works

Per-merchant quartiles, not global thresholds.

A "Top Shopper" for a Taiwanese skincare brand means something completely different from a "Top Shopper" for a global lifestyle merchant. The model uses window functions to compute quartiles within each merchant's customer base — so segments stay merchant-relevant at any scale.

01

Compute per-customer metrics

Recency, frequency, monetary, plus the SHOPLINE-specific interaction score. Successful auths only — failed payments don't count.

02

Rank within merchant

NTILE(4) with PARTITION BY merchant_id. Each merchant's customers are bucketed against their own distribution — solving the "small brand vs global brand" comparison problem.

03

Threshold logic switches by tenure

Mature merchants (>6 months) use their own purchase cycle. New merchants use the category median repurchase cycle as a proxy. Avoids the "no signal" cold start.

04

Classify with CASE rules

The 9 segments are deterministic combinations of R, F, I, M quartiles. Audit-ready and explainable to merchants — which is what makes segments trustworthy.

05

Refresh on a schedule

Daily incremental refresh, full rebuild weekly. Stability matters more than absolute accuracy — merchants need segments that don't whiplash mid-campaign.

-- Smart RFIM segmentation, simplified
WITH customer_metrics AS (
  SELECT
    merchant_id, customer_id,
    COUNT(*) AS frequency,
    SUM(gmv_usd) AS monetary,
    CURRENT_DATE - MAX(order_at)::date AS recency
  FROM orders
  WHERE auth_status = 'success'
  GROUP BY merchant_id, customer_id
),
ranked AS (
  SELECT *,
    NTILE(4) OVER (PARTITION BY merchant_id ORDER BY recency)  AS r_q,
    NTILE(4) OVER (PARTITION BY merchant_id ORDER BY frequency DESC) AS f_q,
    NTILE(4) OVER (PARTITION BY merchant_id ORDER BY monetary DESC)  AS m_q
  FROM customer_metrics
)
SELECT *,
  CASE
    WHEN r_q=1 AND f_q=1 AND m_q=1 THEN 'Top Shopper'
    WHEN r_q<=2 AND f_q<=2 THEN 'Loyal Shopper'
    WHEN r_q>=3 AND f_q>=3 THEN 'Sleeping Customer'
    -- ... 6 more rules
    ELSE 'Engaged Customer'
  END AS rfim_segment
FROM ranked;

Customer Story

One merchant. One week. Half a million in incremental revenue.

Bonny & Read Taiwan-based fashion accessories brand

The Sleeping Customer reactivation play

Bonny & Read had a long tail of customers who'd bought once and gone quiet. Before Smart RFIM, reactivating them meant a marketing manager pulling lists by hand, segmenting in Excel, and crafting bespoke offers — work the team didn't have headcount for.

With Smart RFIM, they segmented the Sleeping cohort by depth of dormancy and lifetime value, then issued differentiated store-credit offers — heavier credit to the highest-value, deepest-sleeping customers. Single workflow, no spreadsheets.

"Don't have to pull customer reports or send store credit by hand any more. Saved a lot of headcount cost. Results are excellent."
